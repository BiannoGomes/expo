**Link format:** `<live-demo-url>/?biz=NOME%20DA%20CLÍNICA` — page personalizes itself.
1. Open THEIR personalized page in front of them (or in the email).
2. Scroll to **"Try it yourself · talk to the receptionist"** → tap **PT** → tap the mic.
3. Say: *"Tenho dor de dentes."* Let them watch it triage, offer amanhã 9h15, take the name, confirm with SMS promise. **Hand them the mic.** This closes harder than any slide.

5. Close to the €97 audit or a 15-min call. Never leave without a date.
