## Identity

- **What it is:** Bianno Gomes's personal brand and book imprint. Documents the real
  transition from deckhand to AI entrepreneur, plus the Self-Mastery books.
- **Audience:** ASK ME (who exactly, and who buys the books today?)
- **Feel (CONFIRMED):** intelligent · deep · disciplined · modern · psychological ·
  philosophical · premium · quietly powerful.
- **Motion principle (CONFIRMED):** *Motion should make an idea feel inevitable.*

## Voice laws (CONFIRMED: content-wave)

- **Zero em-dashes anywhere.** On screen, in captions, in titles.
- No AI-tell words.
- No hashtags on slides or frames. Captions may carry 5 niche hashtags.
- Hooks under 12 words where possible. Angles: curiosity, pain, story, contrarian.
- Captions: hook line, 2–3 short paragraphs, a forward trigger ("send this to someone who is
  searching"), a CTA comment word, 5 niche hashtags.
- CTA comment words: **Book 1 → WARRIOR · Book 2 → OCEAN · Book 3 → CODEX.**

## Source material (CONFIRMED: content-wave)

The books' strongest scenes and lines: Milon's bull · two versions of you · the diver and the
mirror · five years old · the seals · the star. Quote the books only from the manuscript
text. Never paraphrase a quote and present it in quotation marks.

## Never (CONFIRMED: Bianno's brief)

1. Generic self-help graphics or stock motivational visuals.
2. Overused neon gradients.
3. Aggressive masculine clichés.
4. Fake luxury.
5. Empty quotes: every line on screen must carry an idea.

## Sea / NDA rule (CONFIRMED: sea-mode)

Tell the story of **the man, not the boat.** Never show the vessel, its name, deck, interior
or tender. Never show guests, the owner, crew faces, or location tags while he's working.
Before any footage shot at sea is used, ask: *"Does this show the vessel, guests or
location?"* If the answer is yes or unclear, it isn't used. The open sea, sky, horizon, his
own face, hands and writing are safe.
