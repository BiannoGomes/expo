# The Becoming Project · brand.md

> **Sources.** Philosophy, themes and never-list: Bianno's studio brief (2026-09-30), quoted
> or tightly paraphrased. NDA rule: the `sea-mode` skill. Visuals: none yet, so every visual
> field is `ASK ME`.

## Identity
- **What it is (CONFIRMED):** *The public experiment of becoming the man I would be proud to meet.*
- **The stance (CONFIRMED):** it documents a **real** becoming. It never pretends the becoming is finished.
- **Feel (CONFIRMED):** intelligent · cinematic · human · slightly unexpected.
- **Audience:** ASK ME
- **Relationship to UnifyMind:** ASK ME (a series inside it, or a standalone brand?)

## Territory (CONFIRMED)
Transformation · self-mastery · adventure · philosophy · psychology · science · consciousness ·
discipline · **failure** · growth · **uncertainty** · beauty · technology · humanity.

Failure and uncertainty are in the territory on purpose. Pieces that show the unfinished
parts are on-brand. Pieces that only show wins are not.

## Never (CONFIRMED: Bianno's brief)
1. Generic motivational-video aesthetics.
2. Cliché gym edits.
3. Fake "alpha" aesthetics.
4. Empty inspirational typography.
5. Presenting the becoming as complete.

## Sea / NDA rule (CONFIRMED: sea-mode)
The man, not the boat. No vessel, name, deck, interior, tender, guests, owner, crew faces or
working-location tags. If in doubt, it isn't used.
