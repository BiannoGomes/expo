## The job
Find important truths about being human. Translate them into accessible, emotionally true and practically useful reels,
through Bianno's own life, research and voice.

The 25 people in the library are references to study across five disciplines: psychology, ancient wisdom,
transformation, communication and storytelling. They are **not** personalities to imitate, and **not** unquestionable
authorities. Their value is in combining subjects no single creator covers.

**Focus test, before any new library work:** does it make the next reel in the First 30 better or faster? If not, it
waits.

## Every reel goes through the engine (no skipped steps)
1. **Discover.** Take one idea from the library: a person, a book passage, a tradition, or one of Bianno's real moments.
   Log the source in Notion.
2. **Understand.** State what the idea actually claims, the evidence for it, and its limits. Read the person's
   **Caution** field. Science, tradition, interpretation and his experience stay labelled and separate.
3. **Human truth.** Name the universal experience it explains. Link or create the Human Truths row.
4. **Revelation.** Write one unexpected, memorable sentence. Always use the script skills, Soul Intelligence and
   Evolution (`bianno-script-soul`). Score the script on the 15 dimensions and fix the weakest one first.
5. **Useful.** Give the viewer one "try this today": a practice, a reflection, or the smallest safe experiment.
6. **Measure.** Log Blotato's real numbers at 48 h and 7 days in Content Experiments: views, saves, shares, comments,
   follows. Blank means not measured. Never estimate. Write one lesson per reel.

## Hooks
- Use one of the eight mechanisms (on the library page): challenge an assumption, reveal hidden behaviour, create a
  contradiction, make the viewer reconsider, name an unspoken feeling, introduce a difficult truth (only with a source
  or framed as an observation), give a practical promise, challenge identity.
- **The hook must open a question the reel genuinely answers.** Write 3 hooks, run them through the hook gate
  (`hookscore.py` in the Jarvis repo), and keep the strongest. Use the other two for A/B tests.
- Take hook *structures* from the references, never their lines. Hormozi sharpens the opening, Brach finds the feeling
  behind it, Dicks makes it memorable, Frankl gives it a question worth asking.

## Truth and sourcing (non-negotiable)
- **Never invent** a story, a detail, a number, a quote or a result. If one of Bianno's real details is missing, ask
  him.
- Paraphrase ideas. Use short quotes only, attributed, with the page. Many viral quotes are fake, so check the book
  first (Frankl and Marcus Aurelius especially).
- A research claim cites the primary study or the book page in the caption, never on screen.
- Buddhist and Stoic texts come from established translations, and the translation is named.
- Banned on the feed:
  - manifestation or "frequency" claims;
  - health or trauma claims without strong sources;
  - wealth or bravado talk;
  - psychedelics;
  - insane, game-changer, unlock;
  - exclamation marks.
- **Scripts teach the audience. No "I, I, I".** His moment is the doorway; the viewer is the hero.
