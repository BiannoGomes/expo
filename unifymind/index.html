About

The author

Bianno Gomes writes the UnifyMind books.

The work comes out of a childhood in Cape Town, hours lost in drawing, years on the sea, a night in the Matroosberg canyon at fifteen, and Mediterranean crossings watched through to sunrise. Places where attention had nowhere else to go.

Three books so far: Self-Mastery, The Invisible Ocean, and The Sealed Codex. Each one built to be finished, not skimmed.
