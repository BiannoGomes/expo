# Dawnward films: creative direction

Status: proof built, awaiting approval before full production.

## Strategy

| | |
|---|---|
| Audience | 28 to 40, ambitious and busy, has tried journaling or habit apps and quit when the streak broke. |
| Problem | Days blur. You repeat the same patterns (sharp with the people you love when you are tired) without seeing why, and habit apps punish you for missing a day. |
| Recognisable moment | Late at night, replaying something you said. "Snapped at my sister again. Don't know why." |
| Demonstrable transformation | Talk or type one honest minute at night; in the morning, three small steps drawn from your own words; over time, a model of you that shows its reasons and that you can correct. All real flows (see `claims-ledger.md`). |
| Single promise | Your own words, turned into small steps and a picture of you that shows its reasons. |
| Single CTA | Follow the build on Instagram, @bianno.becoming (the only verified next step; no store listing or waitlist exists). |

## Three concepts

### 1. "Your words, back to you" (selected)
Product-led and typographic. The film is the life of one sentence. A confession typed on black ("Snapped at my sister again. Don't know why.") flies into the real evening debrief; overnight its key word travels to the morning step ("Call your sister. Just to talk."); later the same thread returns as an insight with its reason and the real "That's not right". Signature motifs: words that travel between real screens on matched vectors; the app's own time-of-day tones (evening amber, predawn indigo, dawn) as the only light; full-screen serif words on black for the few statements that matter. Music carries, the voice is sparse, interface sounds are tiny and real.
- Why it can win: every frame is the product, and the emotional beat (being understood, and being allowed to say "that's not right") is a real feature, not a metaphor.
- Risk: restraint can read as quiet. Mitigation: a hard, human first two seconds (the typed confession) and precise motion that lands on narrative beats.

### 2. "Four worlds"
World-building spectacle. The camera travels through a crafted universe where Mind, Body, Soul and Future bloom from the user's days, with the phone as a window. Closest to the earlier Living Universe film.
- Why it could work: distinctive, beautiful, ownable imagery.
- Why it loses: the earlier reviews showed the planets read as wallpaper and push comprehension to the back; the strongest product truth (steps from your words, insights with reasons) becomes small.

### 3. "The night before the call"
Character-led 2D animation in the spirit of character studios: one illustrated person, one evening, one regret, one morning phone call, with the app appearing at the two moments it matters.
- Why it could work: the most emotional; a human face makes the problem instant.
- Why it loses now: needs original character design and frame-by-frame animation to avoid looking generated, which this pipeline cannot deliver at the required craft in time; a fictional person also invites testimonial-like readings.

## 60 s flagship (concept 1), outline for approval

| Time | Beat | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Hook | Black. The confession types itself in Source Serif. | Keys, room tone, the score's first notes |
| 2 to 8 | Cost | "Don't know why." Hold. A second line of the same night ("Said I'd call. Didn't.") | Silence then breath |
| 8 to 16 | Shift | The words fly into the real debrief; "Dawnward is a journal you talk to." | VO, interface ticks |
| 16 to 24 | Demo 1 | Debrief slides to Today; "sister" travels into "Call your sister. Just to talk." | VO "Every morning, three small steps. Drawn from your own words." |
| 24 to 30 | Demo 2 | Full-screen words on black: "No streaks." / "No scores." / "No guilt." one per spoken phrase | VO, three soft hits |
| 30 to 36 | Demo 3 | The Personal Model: the same thread returns as an insight, its reason in the app's words; "That's not right" sets it aside at your word | VO "Slowly, it understands you. And shows you why." / "And when it's wrong, you can tell it." |
| 36 to 48 | Resolution | Slower. Dawn light reaches the phone; the four worlds glimpsed as the morning's quiet hero image; "Private. Never sold." | Score opens, voice rests |
| 48 to 56 | Promise | "Your own words, back to you." on black, then "Just you, understood." | Score peak, then a held note |
| 56 to 60 | Lockup and CTA | Dawnward, "Become who you are becoming.", @bianno.becoming, "Coming soon. In private testing." | Sonic logo: one soft chime |

Cutdowns: 15 s conversion cut (hook, the word travelling into the step, "No streaks. No scores. No guilt.", the insight with its reason, lockup and CTA) and a 6 s hook (the confession, the word landing in the step, wordmark).
