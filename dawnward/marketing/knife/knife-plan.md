# The Knife Plan · Dawnward on Instagram

The method, in one line: pick the one platform where your people already are, name their problem better than they can, agree with them, and talk to them. Do not sell. The conversations will change the product, and the product that comes out of them sells itself.

Claude does the making. Bianno does the talking. Nothing publishes or sends without Bianno's yes.

---

**Instagram, @bianno.becoming.** Verified connected in Blotato (account 75385). It already carries the Becoming Project voice, it already has two live keyword-DM flows (SPOTLIGHT, MIDDLE), and its audience is the Dawnward customer: people trying to become someone, quietly.

## 2. The person we're talking to

These are hypotheses, not facts yet. Week 1 starts testing them.

- Late 20s to early 40s, busy, competent, privately unsure where it's all going.
- Has tried journaling, habit apps, morning routines; has restarted all of them more than once.
- Is tired of being graded by apps (streaks, scores, red dots) and tired of motivational content that shouts.
- Wants to be understood more than they want to be optimised.

## 3. The problem bank (in their words, not ours)

## 4. What gets made (zero product mentions, weeks 1-8)

Four formats, each built to start a conversation, not to perform:

1. **Name-it reels (7-15 s).** One sentence on screen that the viewer has thought but never said, a slow dark visual, the Becoming Project look. Ends on a question. Produced with the `becoming-video` pipeline; voice optional (Bianno's cloned voice or none).
2. **Affirm carousels (5-7 slides).** "The thing nobody tells you about restarting." Validates, never prescribes a product. Blotato design templates or the existing carousel system.
3. **Question posts.** One plain question as the whole post. The comments are the research.
4. **Daily story sticker.** A poll or question sticker. Takes 10 seconds to answer, feeds the problem bank.

**Cadence:** 1 reel or carousel per day, plus 1 story per day. That is 7 feed posts a week, all made in one batch.

## 5. The conversation engine (this is the knife)

- **Every comment gets a human reply within 24 hours.** Claude drafts suggested replies from the comments Blotato pulls in; Bianno edits and sends. Replies ask one follow-up question; they never pitch.
- **One keyword flow, conversation not lead-magnet:** comment **TELL** → DM: "One question, and I read every answer: what's the thing you keep restarting, and what usually stops you?" Replies land in his inbox for real conversation. (Drafted below; not live until Bianno says yes.)
- **Warm only.** No cold DMs, ever: we only talk to people who spoke first.

## 6. The learning loop (how the product changes)

Every Sunday, Claude:
1. Pulls the week's comments, DMs and story answers (Blotato comments and conversations).
2. Clusters them into problems, quoting exact phrases.
3. Updates the problem bank: new rows, retired rows, their words replacing ours.
4. Writes a one-page **product signal** note for Dawnward: what people asked for, what they hated, what surprised us. That note feeds the Dawnward backlog.
5. Drafts next week's 7 posts from the strongest new language.

Bianno spends 10 minutes approving the batch. Blotato schedules it.

## 9. What we measure

**Primary: conversations per week** (comment threads with a reply back, DM threads). Then saves and shares (the "this is me" signal), then waitlist joins from week 5. Not follower count, not likes.

## 10. Rules this plan obeys

- AI proposes, Bianno approves anything that posts, sends or spends.
- Warm only; no cold outreach.
- No new tools, agents or trackers: this runs on Blotato, the existing becoming-video pipeline and the publish-ops routine.
- The Human Law: no em-dashes, no AI-sounding language, no guilt, no streaks or scores in our own content.
- Never invent numbers or testimonials; every claim about the audience comes from what they actually said.
