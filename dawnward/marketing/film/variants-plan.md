# Dawnward film v4 · Phase 12 commercial testing plan

The master film establishes the brand; shorter cuts test different reasons to care. All variants share the same identity and the same byte-identical three-second sign-off (wordmark + the completed dawn motif), per the campaign-grammar finding in the research library.

## Measurement constraints (honest)

- No live destination exists yet, so click-through and conversion cannot be measured until the owner deploys the site or a waitlist. Until then the testable metrics are retention, completion, saves, shares and profile visits from the platforms' own analytics.
- Secondary metrics: profile visits, follows, comment comprehension ("what does this app do" asked vs answered).
- Confounders to respect: posting time, first-frame thumbnail differences, sound-on vs sound-off exposure (captions are burned into none of the variants; platform captions from `captions-v4.srt` should be attached), and audience overlap between tests.
- Sample rule: no winner declared under roughly 1,000 impressions per variant on the same placement, and observed differences are correlations, not causal proof.

## What is deliberately not produced

Every hook x sound x treatment combination. The three hooks above each isolate one variable against the same sign-off; more combinations before the first real data would be noise.
