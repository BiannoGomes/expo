# Dawnward film v4 · Phase 2 creative concepts

Three genuinely different concepts, scored, with a selected winner. The audience hypotheses behind them (people who feel busy but directionless; serial restarters; people who want growth without gamification; people whose daily actions feel disconnected from their ambitions) are hypotheses drawn from the product's own design documents and the positioning research, not validated customer facts.

## Concept 1 · "The Quiet Hour" (intimate human truth)

- **One sentence**: at the end of an ordinary, noisy day, one honest minute with yourself becomes the way you finally see where your life is going.
- **Tension**: your day is full and loud, and none of it tells you whether you're moving toward anything.
- **Hook**: darkness, a face-down phone, notification sounds stacking faster and faster, then a hard cut to total silence.
- **Arc**: noise → silence → one honest sentence → a star → an accumulating sky → invitation.
- **Product demo**: the evening debrief carries the whole middle.
- **Visual device**: the film itself performs the product's promise: it goes quiet when Dawnward appears.
- **Sound concept**: the loudest sound design in the film is the problem; the product lives in near-silence.
- **Brand moment**: wordmark over a dark sky with a single gold star.
- **Conversion hypothesis**: recognition drives saves and shares among overstimulated self-improvers.
- **Risks / why it might fail**: a quiet film in a muted feed loses its central contrast; the slow middle risks drop-off at 10-20 s; shows less of the app's breadth.
- **Cost**: low. Mostly existing assets plus one new opening clip.

## Concept 2 · "First Light" (cinematic transformation)

- **One sentence**: one evening reflection travels outward from a typed sentence until a whole life becomes a navigable universe.
- **Tension**: you can't see your own life from inside it.
- **Hook**: extreme close-up of a thumb typing one honest sentence; the final word lifts off the glass as light.
- **Arc**: a single continuous camera journey: glass → word → star → orbit → worlds → galaxy → pull back to the phone in a hand.
- **Product demo**: weaker; screens appear as waypoints rather than demonstrations.
- **Visual device**: one unbroken move; the phone screen is a window the camera physically passes through.
- **Sound concept**: a single swelling cue with no cuts, Vision-Pro style.
- **Brand moment**: the pullback reveals the whole journey happened inside the phone.
- **Conversion hypothesis**: spectacle drives completion and rewatch.
- **Risks / why it might fail**: comprehension suffers (beautiful but what does it do?); continuous-move 2.5D is the hardest build; closest to generic "AI space film" territory if the craft slips.
- **Cost**: high. New footage, heaviest engineering.

## Concept 3 · "What You Live Becomes Matter" (interface and cosmos in one mechanic)

- **One sentence**: everything real you do in Dawnward visibly becomes matter in your universe: words become stars, mornings become orbits, weeks become worlds.
- **Tension**: your effort disappears; nothing you do accumulates into anything you can see.
- **Hook**: a real sentence typing in the debrief; the caret pauses; the film holds its breath.
- **Arc**: paired moments, cut as rhymes: kept task → star ignites; honest sentence → light leaves the glass; a week of mornings → a world gains weather; the model's observation → a constellation line draws. Then one earned reveal of the whole.
- **Product demo**: the strongest: every cosmic image is caused by a shown, real interface event, so the mechanic (your words → understanding → your universe) teaches itself.
- **Visual device**: the match cut as the signature; UI micro-event to cosmic macro-event.
- **Sound concept**: each pair shares one sound (the chime belongs to both the task and the star), building a sonic vocabulary.
- **Brand moment**: "Become who you are becoming." after the last pair resolves.
- **Conversion hypothesis**: product comprehension plus originality drives qualified clicks, not just views.
- **Risks / why it might fail**: match cuts demand frame-precise craft; if a pair reads as decoration rather than cause-and-effect the whole argument weakens; needs more distinct shots than v3.
- **Cost**: medium. Reuses the pipeline; needs one or two new clips and a rebuilt timeline.

## Scoring (1-10, comparative judgement, not evidence)

| Criterion | C1 Quiet Hour | C2 First Light | C3 Becomes Matter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audience relevance | 9 | 6 | 8 |
| Originality | 7 | 6 | 9 |
| Product comprehension | 6 | 4 | 9 |
| Emotional resonance | 9 | 7 | 8 |
| Visual distinctiveness | 7 | 9 | 9 |
| Sound potential | 9 | 7 | 8 |
| Memorability | 7 | 7 | 9 |
| Production feasibility | 9 | 5 | 7 |
| Conversion clarity | 6 | 5 | 9 |
| **Total** | **69** | **56** | **76** |

Why: C1 wins on feeling but under-demonstrates the product and depends on sound in a sound-off medium. C2 is the most spectacular and the least commercial; it is also the concept most at risk of looking like every AI space montage. C3 is the only concept where the beauty IS the product explanation: each image is caused by a real interface event, which satisfies the human, product and mythology layers simultaneously.

## Selection

**Winner: Concept 3**, strengthened with the best organ of each loser:
- From C1: the noise-to-silence opening (the problem is loud, Dawnward is quiet) and the strategic use of silence.
- From C2: one single through-the-screen camera move, reserved for the universe reveal only, so the film's biggest gesture is spent once, where it is earned.

The final film is therefore: a loud, recognisable problem → silence → the honest minute (debrief) → the first pair (sentence becomes star) → the morning proof (quiet moves, no gamification) → suspension → through the glass into the universe (the one big move) → the worlds as consequences → the payoff (it remembers who you're becoming) → brand → honest invitation.
