# Design references

- `dawnward-universe-reference.png` (1536 × 1024): Bianno's "Infinite Universe" concept board, made with ChatGPT and supplied in the Claude Code session on 2026-10-31. It's the primary visual direction for the Universe, the galaxies, the five worlds, the mission, the Quantum Jump and Return to Earth.
- **It's a direction, not a spec.** Words and numbers in it are illustrative. Nothing in it is shipped as-is: its art is reproduced only through assets Bianno supplies, recorded in `experience/assets/img/worlds/SOURCE.md`.

## dawnward-home-concept-early.png

Bianno's earlier home concept (supplied 2026-10-31, made with ChatGPT). Visual reference only. What the product adopted:
- planets that glow in their world colour, readable names, a central light;
- a calm "speak or write" entry point (now the Reflect tab and Home's reflection card).

What it deliberately does not copy:
- **"You've made progress… a calmer mindset" card.** The app shows only what was done and kept, never a judgement it can't back up.
- **Four worlds.** There are five now, with Spirit separate from Soul.
- **Repeated Explore buttons.** Each world's name is its button.
- **Time-of-day greetings.**

## quantum-portal-reference.png

## mindfinity-board.png

"MINDFINITY / Dawnward: Cinematic Multiverse Engine" board (supplied 2026-10-31). Direction for:
- the world identities (Crystal Expanse, Living Wilds, Valley of Connection, Observatory of Being, Horizon Citadel);
- the galaxies;
- the Living Library;
- the Memory Constellations;
- the AI Expedition Guide.

Its numbers ("Day 4 of 14", "28%", "5 Missions") are illustrative and are not shown in the app unless real data produces them.
