# Chapter art, round 2: provenance

Made by Bianno Gomes with ChatGPT image generation, 2026-10-31, from `dawnward/docs/universe/chatgpt-copy-paste-in-order.txt`. Supplied in the Claude Code session as 87 PNGs in five batches, unnamed; matched to their prompt codes by eye. Converted to WebP with ImageMagick: portraits 720 px wide, headers 1280, orbs 640, pathway planets 512, moons 384.

**Moons and transparent pieces (`-K`, T9, T10):** ChatGPT's "transparent" cut-outs had a jagged red and yellow fringe at the edge. Their alpha was pulled in by 9 px and softened (ImageMagick: erode Disk:9, blur 4). No other edits.

**Spirit:** S1 replaced `../worlds/planet-spirit-640.webp` and S2 replaced `../worlds/closeup-spirit.webp`; the crystal placeholders are gone. Its globe sits at centre (0.491, 0.49), radius 0.423, measured with `-trim`.

**This folder** holds what the app shows now (inlined in the single-file preview). `library/` holds the rest, ready for the Quantum Gateway journey, the planned chapters and the interface kit.

**Choices:**
- Two Knowledge orbs came back. A4 (blue and gold) is used because it matches the brief's palette and stands apart from the violet Spiritual and Creative orbs; the violet one is kept as `library/A4-alt.webp`.
- U5 and U7 were made on a white background, not transparent. Remake them before use.
- The interface kit isn't used as images. Panels and buttons stay as styled HTML, so text stays sharp, readable and accessible; the kit guides their look.
