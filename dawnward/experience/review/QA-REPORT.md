## Independent reviews

Two panels reviewed the first revision. Story/film/sound: Creative 6, Film 5, Sound 5, Customer 5. Experience: Motion 6, Product fidelity 5, Accessibility 6. Their blockers and top repairs were all addressed in commit 5c8ec23c and the re-render:

- Unlabelled personal content (Soul panel in the film, text version images): labelled; the text version now uses corrected screens.
- Invented evidence wording ("in your evenings 3 times this month"): replaced everywhere with the server's own "I have seen this in your days 3 times now."
- The core mechanic never spoken: new line at 6.75 s; Soul narrated and shortened; phone returns on the climax; dispute shows the app's real "Set aside, at your word" state; end card on a downbeat with the CTA on the accent.
- Captions over UI text: now on a plate. Voice masked by music: now 6 to 19 dB over. Effects inaudible: now audible, under the music.
- World panel order and dispute: matches `app/world/[world].tsx`, with the app's reply.
- Scroll feel: critically damped spring; multi-chapter jumps cross-fade; real time kept down to 10 fps.
- Contrast over planets, missing page heading, Label in Name on Sound, nav under the open panel, captions missing at rests: fixed.
