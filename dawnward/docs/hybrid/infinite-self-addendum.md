# The Infinite Self: addendum to the hybrid plan

**Status:** proposal. Written 2026-10-31 from Bianno's "Infinite Self" concept and its 16-screen board. It **updates** `hybrid-product-architecture.md` and `game-design-document.md`; where they differ, this file wins.

## What it changes

1. **Five worlds, not four (D-H2 answered by Bianno).**
   - Mind, Body, Soul, **Spirit** and Future.
   - Soul is relationships, values, emotional connection and meaning. Spirit is awe, contemplation, stillness, wonder and philosophical questions, as personal experience.
   - Spirit needs its own art: a planet and a close-up, matching the other four.
2. **The narrative arc:** Present → Explore → Imagine → Choose → Act → Return.
3. **Names:**
   - **Inner Galaxy:** personal growth, your present reality. Was "Your Galaxy".
   - **Discovery Galaxy:** practice, missions and experiments. Was "Expedition Galaxy".
   - **Earth Realm:** the gateway to your present life, beneath the five worlds.
4. **The Quantum Gateway and the Future Self Observatory** replace the plain "step forward". Their three objects are the Mirror, the Crossroads and the First Step.
5. **Return to Earth** is the end of every journey: one real action, saved, and a memory star appears.

## The Quantum Gateway, built honestly

- **The visual:**
  - A ring of light forms around Earth.
  - The camera approaches the portal; stars stretch into trails, and the universe folds inward.
  - **The first trip:** about 3 seconds.
  - **Repeat trips:** about 1 second.
  - **Always:** skippable, and interruptible by Back or Escape.
  - **Reduced motion:** a cross-fade.
- **The words:**
  - Physics images (wormholes, time dilation, superposition) are used as metaphor only.
  - The copy never says the experience jumps realities, predicts the future or guarantees change.
  - The arrival line: "A possible future, built from what you told me you want. Not a prediction."
- **The Mirror:**
  - You talk with a future-self perspective built from your own `future_selves` words, quoted.
  - The AI can ask questions in that voice. It can't claim facts about your future, and it says "I don't know" when the evidence is missing.
- **The Crossroads:**
  - Two or three paths you name.
  - For each path: the assumptions it rests on, the trade-offs, and what you'd need to learn first.
  - The decision stays yours.
- **The First Step:** one small action this week. It becomes an experiment with a check-in, then you return to Earth.

## Scope: what the board shows that I'd defer, and why

The board also shows AI Creation, Knowledge, Spiritual Expansion and Creative Worlds galaxies.

| Board item | Recommendation | Reason |

| AI Creation Galaxy (agents, automation, business) | **Leave out** | A different product. It pulls Dawnward away from private reflection and growth. |
| Knowledge Galaxy (physics, history, nature) | Defer | Courses are a large content commitment. Revisit after the beta. |
| Creative Worlds | Defer | Same. It could become a Spirit or Future system later (creative practice). |
| Spiritual Expansion: meditation realms, ancient wisdom | **Fold into Spirit** | Contemplation and awe practices fit the Spirit world |
| "Astral Projection", "Quantum Consciousness", "Multi-Dimensional Realms" | **Leave out as named** | These read as factual claims. The App Store and your own trust rules both need anything health-adjacent to stay clearly reflective. Possible replacements: "Awe practice", "Questions about consciousness", "Perspective shifts". |
| "World progress, Level 8" bar; "Your evolution" chart rising over time | **Depends on D-H1** | Your brand brief forbids levels and progress bars that measure the person. Plain counts of things done ("128 reflections, 24 missions, 18 actions, 7 discoveries") are facts and could stay. A rising "evolution" line implies a measure of you. |
| Daily Compass | Keep, as the Today screen | It's the existing three morning steps, shown around Earth |
