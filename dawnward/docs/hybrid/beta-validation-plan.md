# Beta validation plan

**Question:** does the hybrid experience help people take useful real-world action more than reflection alone? Does it do so without hurting the quiet reflection people came for?

## Design

- **Two arms**, assigned at random at sign-up:
  - **R:** reflection-only, today's app;
  - **H:** hybrid, with the Explore tab and the Soul · Communication system.
- **Pilot size:** 20 to 30 people per arm for 3 weeks. Enough to find usability problems and rough effect sizes, not to prove benefit. A pilot cannot establish long-term psychological effects, and nothing will claim it does.
- **Recruitment:** adults, from Dawnward's waitlist, with informed consent. Excluded if currently in crisis: the onboarding safety screen applies. Participants can withdraw and delete everything at any time.

## Measures

| Measure | How | Target (proposed, not a benchmark) |
|---|---|---|
| Mission comprehension | After mission 1: "What were you practising?" (free text, coded by two people) | ≥ 90% correct |
| Introductory mission completion | Events | ≥ 70% of those who begin |
| Perceived usefulness | 1 to 5 after each mission | Average ≥ 4 |
| Real-world follow-up | Experiment check-ins: did, partly or didn't (self-report) | ≥ 40% of those who plan one report trying |
| Time to first reflection | Events | No slower in H than in R |
| Reflection continuity | Reflections per week | No drop in H vs R (the game must not replace reflecting) |
| Voluntary return | Days active in week 3 | Reported, not targeted. Engagement is not benefit. |
| Trust | "I feel in control of what Dawnward concludes about me" (1 to 5) | H ≥ R |
| Navigation | Critical failures in observed sessions | Zero |
| Data | Confirmed lost entries | Zero |
| Accessibility | Critical barriers in core journeys | Zero |

## Method notes

- **Five observed sessions per arm** (think-aloud), plus short interviews in week 3.
- **Privacy:** analytics are counts only, never content.
- **Decision rule:**
  - **Go:** comprehension, data, navigation and accessibility targets met, and reflection continuity not worse.
  - **Iterate:** usefulness or follow-up below target.
  - **Stop:** trust or reflection continuity clearly worse in H.
