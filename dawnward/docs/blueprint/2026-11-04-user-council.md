# The user council: 10 experienced users debate Dawnward (4 Nov 2026)

How this was made: a scripted newcomer walk-through of the real build at 390 px (first open → the suggested practice to Done → every tab), with the screen text, the tappable things on each screen and screenshots recorded. The ten people below are personas, written to argue from what that walk-through showed. They are not real testers. Every claim is from the build; nothing is guessed.

## What the walk-through measured
- First screen: **18 tappable things**, and **3 different ways to start writing** ("What's on your mind?", the Reflect tab, five world planets), plus "Set a direction", which isn't explained.
- The suggested practice is 7 steps. Before you write a word about your own situation, you get an intro (133 words), a 2-minute lesson (273 words) and a sorting exercise. **Your own words come at step 4 of 7.**
- Inside a practice there are **three "Home" links at once** (breadcrumb, "← Home", the tab bar), and the tab bar lights up "Learn" even though you started from Home.
- After Chapter 1, **Chapter 2 is marked "Plus"**, and membership isn't open. Home then switches to a different series (The Thought Labyrinth). The decision story stops after 8 minutes.
- At the same moment, Home shows three things: "Knowledge is open", the next practice and "How did it go?".
- Universe on day one: **6 of 7 galaxies locked**, with "0 of 3" counters. One of them is still called "AI Creation".
- Journey has 4 views plus a filter. You talks about "Your plan: Free" and "Preview as a member".
- **Bugs found and fixed today:**
  - Home said "Continue · 1 of 7" while you were really on step 6.
  - The word "null" was printed on an empty You page and on an empty Journey page.

  Tests now cover all three.

## Round 1: first impressions (score /10)
- **Maya (6):** "The first line, 'Going back and forth on something?', is me. I tapped Begin instantly. Then it gave me a lesson about loops. I came with *my* decision and it made me read about *someone else's* job application for three screens. When it finally asked about mine (step 4), it was good. Really good. But I nearly left at the lesson."
- **Dev (5):** "Where is the output? I finished, and the one thing I got out of it was the action I typed myself. Fine, that's honest. But the app has 7 galaxies, 5 worlds, 5 tabs, 4 Journey views and 'Expeditions', 'Chapters', 'Missions', 'Practices', 'Parts'. That's five words for one thing. I can't build a mental model."
- **Ruth (4):** "Pretty pictures. I didn't know what to press. Three things said Home. 'Set a direction': a direction for what? The planets are lovely, but I don't know what Soul is for."
- **Sam (5):** "First screen is a vibe, I'll give it that. But after Chapter 1 it says 'Plus' and I can't buy Plus. So the thing it promised me, stop overthinking, ends after 8 minutes? That's a bait and switch, even if you didn't mean it."
- **Dr Lena (7):** "The content is the best thing here. The sorting exercise, 'Moves forward / Goes round', with a kind explanation even when you 'get it wrong', is textbook good practice. 'Nothing here is scored.' 'Not yet is fine.' No streaks. The safety line is on the intro. My worry is the opposite of everyone's: don't cut the psychoeducation. Just stop making it a wall."
- **Jonas (6):** "It speaks properly. Labels are real sentences, focus lands on titles, and the tiles are radio buttons. But on Home I tab through 18 stops before anything, and the locked galaxies each read out a whole paragraph of rules. That's a lot of listening to learn what I *can't* do."
- **Priya (8):** "On-device only, no account, no tracking, no AI. That's rare and real. Two things undercut it: the web Speak button (Chrome sends audio to Google), and a 'Your plan' section implying accounts and payment that don't exist."
- **Marcus (6):** "The universe is the soul of this thing. But I can't play it on day one: 6 of 7 locked, with '0 of 3' progress bars. That's a grind meter, not wonder. In Journey, the game is to discover. Here the game is to qualify."
- **Aisha (5):** "8 minutes is too long for my day, and the first 3 of them are reading. I want a 2-minute version on busy days. And I'd only come back if something reminded me, on my terms."
- **Tom (4):** "Every app I deleted looked gorgeous in week one. What makes me open this on day 9? Right now it's a calendar file I have to save myself, and a Week page that's empty until I've written 3+ times. The payoff comes after the effort, and most people quit before that."

**Average: 5.6 / 10.** Strong content and an honest privacy story, buried under too many places, words and gates.

## Round 2: the debates

### Debate 1: lesson first, or my words first?
- **Maya:** "Ask me first. 'What are you going back and forth on?' Then teach me using *my* sentence."
- **Dr Lena:** "If you skip the explanation, people write their loop and stay in it. The lesson is the treatment."
- **Sam:** "Then make the lesson 3 lines and put 'Why this works' behind a tap."
- **Lena (conceding):** "Fine. If the sorting exercise uses *their* thoughts instead of the job example, it's actually *better* practice. Personal material sticks."
- **Verdict (9–1):** your decision first, sorting your own thoughts second, the lesson as a short "why", the full reading optional.

### Debate 2: the universe. Keep it, cut it, or hide it?
- **Ruth, Dev:** "Cut it. It's decoration that makes me lost."
- **Marcus:** "Cut it and you're one of 400 journaling apps. It's the only thing anyone will remember."
- **Tom:** "Remembering isn't returning."
- **Marcus:** "Then make it the *reward*, not the *menu*. Your reflections become stars and your universe grows. Show it after I do something, not as a place I have to navigate."
- **Priya:** "That also means nothing is locked behind 'qualify by doing 3 practices'. It just grows."
- **Verdict (8–2):** keep the universe as **your progress made visible**, not as the main navigation. No locks or counters on day one. Hide galaxies until they open, with one line: "More of your universe appears as you go." Rename "AI Creation".

### Debate 3: five tabs
- **Dev:** "Home, Universe, Reflect, Learn, Journey is two too many. Universe and Journey both show my universe. Home and Learn both suggest a practice."
- **Jonas:** "Fewer tabs, please. Every tab is a stop."
- **Marcus:** "Merge Universe into Journey. Your Journey *is* your universe."
- **Verdict (10–0):** **three tabs: Today · Reflect · Journey.**
  - Learn becomes "All practices" inside Today.
  - The Universe map moves into Journey.
  - You stays behind the profile icon.

### Debate 4: membership ("Plus")
- **Sam:** "Chapter 2 is locked, and there's no way to pay. Worst of both."
- **Dev:** "You'll want revenue one day."
- **Priya:** "One day. Today it's a beta with no payments. Show nothing about plans."
- **Tom:** "And when you do charge, never lock the middle of a story someone started."
- **Verdict (10–0):** in the beta, **all four Beyond Overthinking chapters are free**. Remove "Your plan" and "Preview as a member" from You until membership really exists.

### Debate 5: what brings me back?
- **Aisha:** "A weekly moment, not a daily one."
- **Tom:** "And a reason to write before the payoff, like a promise of what it'll show me."
- **Lena:** "Honest progress: 'Write 2 more and Dawnward can start noticing what keeps coming up.' That's motivating without being a streak."
- **Dev:** "And the Week page should offer a Sunday calendar reminder, the same way 'Remind me' works."
- **Verdict (9–1):**
  - **Sunday "Your week"** is the return ritual, with a one-tap recurring calendar reminder;
  - an honest "x more reflections until noticing starts" line;
  - a **2-minute version** of practices for busy days.

### Debate 6: one thing at a time
- **Ruth:** "After I finished, Home showed me three things. Which one?"
- **Verdict (10–0):**
  - Home shows **one** card, in this order: "How did it go?" if something is waiting, then your series' next chapter, then the suggested practice.
  - Unlock news moves to Journey.
  - Inside a practice, hide the tab bar and show one ✕ Close (no breadcrumb, no "← Home").

### Where they still disagree
- **Marcus vs Ruth on the 3D:** Ruth wants a plain mode that's still beautiful. Marcus wants the 3D on by default. Compromise: the art stays; the 3D only plays on Journey and Universe, never on Today.
- **Lena vs Sam on the safety line:** Lena wants it on every intro. Sam says it reads like a warning label. Compromise: keep it once, on the first practice, and inside "You › Support" always.

## The chairman's verdict
**Dawnward should do one job brilliantly: "Stop going round in circles on a decision, in your own words, on your own phone."** The content already does this job at a 7–8. The app around it does it at a 4–5, because it makes people learn a world before it helps them.

The 10 changes, in order of impact:
1. **Your decision first.** Chapter 1 opens with "What are you going back and forth on?"; the sorting exercise uses your own thoughts; the lesson becomes 3 lines plus "Why this works".
2. **All 4 Beyond Overthinking chapters free.** Remove plan and membership talk from the beta.
3. **One card on Today.** "How did it go?" → your next chapter → a suggestion. Nothing else above the fold except "What's on your mind?".
4. **Three tabs: Today · Reflect · Journey.** Universe moves into Journey; Learn becomes "All practices".
5. **Focus mode inside a practice.** No tab bar, one ✕ Close, no duplicate Home links.
6. **No day-one locks.** Galaxies appear as they open, with no counters or rules. Rename "AI Creation".
7. **Honest promise of the payoff.** "Write 2 more and Dawnward can start noticing…" on Reflect and Journey.
8. **Sunday "Your week" ritual,** with a recurring calendar reminder (made on the device, like Remind me).
9. **A 2-minute version** of each chapter for busy days.
10. **One vocabulary.** "Practices" grouped into "series" (or "chapters"). Retire "Expedition", "Mission" and "Part" from anything people read.

Expected effect, according to the council: from 5.6 to about 7.5 before any new content. Content isn't the bottleneck; the path to it is.
