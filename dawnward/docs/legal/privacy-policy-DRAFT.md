## 2. Without an account

Without an account, everything — reflections, progress and creations — stays in the browser on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

You can export everything as a file and delete everything at any time from inside the app. Clearing browser storage, changing devices or uninstalling software may remove locally stored content. Keep an export if you want a separate copy.

## 3. With an account

Your reflections are stored on our server: Railway, EU region, using Postgres. Reflections are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to generate a reply. If you use voice recordings, those recordings go to ElevenLabs for transcription.

These are our only third-party providers. We have no advertising or tracking and do not sell your data.

## 4. Purposes and lawful basis

We process account information and reflections to provide the account service you request, store reflections and generate replies. The proposed lawful basis is performance of our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, where processing is objectively necessary for that service.

Voice transcription is optional. The proposed basis for sending recordings for transcription is your consent, Article 6(1)(a). You can stop using voice and withdraw consent through the privacy contact without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Reflections or recordings may reveal health, religion, sexual orientation or other sensitive information. Where Article 9 GDPR applies, we propose to obtain your separate, explicit consent before collecting or processing that information, under Article 9(2)(a). Accepting the Terms alone is not explicit consent. If you decline or withdraw it, affected account features cannot process that sensitive content; account-free use remains available.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and data portability, including direct transfer where technically feasible. You may object where an objection right applies, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact us using the details above.

We normally respond within one month. A permitted extension of up to two further months will be explained within the first month. We may request proportionate identity verification. Requests are normally free.

You can complain to Spain’s [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es/) or another competent supervisory authority, including where you live or work.

## 8. Age, support and changes

Dawnward is for people aged 16 or over. It shows crisis-support information when someone writes about self-harm. It is not therapy or medical advice. Some content is philosophy or tradition, labelled as such, rather than scientific fact.

We will date updates and explain material changes inside the app before they take effect where required. New purposes requiring consent will need fresh consent.
