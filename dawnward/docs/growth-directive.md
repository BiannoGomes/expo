# Dawnward growth directive (owner-adopted 2026-10-24)

This is the working strategy document Bianno adopted for taking Dawnward from finished product to category of its own. Where anything here conflicts with `design-direction.md` or `brand-brief.md`, those remain canon. User-facing copy lifted from this document is normalized to the Human Law before it ships (no em-dashes, no AI-telling language).

## The brand north star

**Become who you are becoming.**

Three interconnected ideas carry the brand:

1. **Personal understanding.** Dawnward remembers what matters to you, rather than treating you as a collection of habits.
2. **Meaningful direction.** It helps identify the one thing that most needs your attention now.
3. **A life you can recognise.** Progress is revealed through real experiences, never scores.

## The competitive distinction

| Typical growth app | Dawnward |
| --- | --- |
| Tracks habits | Understands the person |
| Gives everyone similar routines | Adapts to the individual's circumstances |
| Encourages streaks and scores | Recognises meaningful lived moments |
| Focuses on today's checklist | Connects today to a longer personal direction |
| Shows generic AI responses | Builds on the person's own words and history |

The product and marketing must demonstrate these, not merely claim them.

## Visual direction

The established system holds: nocturnal world, near-black `#0B0E12`, restrained gold `#C9A96A`, Cormorant Garamond display, Albert Sans body, IBM Plex Mono labels, three sanctioned motion moments. Visual sophistication comes from composition, typography, light, depth and meaning, never from adding motion everywhere. The four signature surfaces: the Living Sky, editorial intelligence, the personal constellation, and the first light (milestone payoffs).

## Component sources (21st.dev and similar registries)

Use open component registries as implementation references only. Inspect source, licensing, dependencies, accessibility and mobile behavior before adapting anything, and rework every borrowed idea into the Dawnward tokens. Reject attractive components that weaken identity, accessibility, performance, privacy or clarity.

## Landing page structure (implemented in site/index.html)

1. Emotional hook: "You don't need another routine. You need a direction."
2. The problem: people collect advice but nothing they use knows them.
3. The alternative: know yourself, find your direction, move forward.
4. The product, shown with real screens: invitation, Today, debrief, witnessed, reveal, Future Self.
5. The difference: understood, not measured.
6. Privacy, in plain language.
7. One ask, matching the real onboarding.

The page must never depend on an abstract cinematic animation; a visitor should understand what Dawnward does within seconds.

## Marketing channels

| Channel | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram and TikTok | Recognition and curiosity |
| YouTube | Trust and depth |
| Search | Capture existing intent |
| App Store and Google Play | Convert intent into installs |
| Email | Bring interested people back |
| In-product sharing | Voluntary advocacy (a constellation the user chooses to share) |

Marketing never suggests Dawnward diagnoses a person, knows them perfectly, or guarantees transformation. The evidence-led personal model is the trust proposition.

## Growth experiments, in order

- **A. Positioning**: "Understand yourself more deeply" vs "Find your next meaningful step" vs "Become who you are becoming." Metric: landing visitors who begin onboarding.
- **B. Product demonstration**: cinematic constellation-led preview vs practical walkthrough. Metric: onboarding starts per visitor.
- **C. Onboarding**: introductory and consent wording variants, never weakening the privacy choice. Metric: completion, with comprehension and abandonment reasons as guardrails.
- **D. First meaningful value**: Future Self vs bottleneck vs first campaign. Metric: engagement with the first recommendation.
- **E. Retention**: do the debrief and weekly reveal bring people back because they find it valuable? Metric: D7/D30 plus debrief completion and qualitative feedback.

Baseline first, one variable at a time, report sample sizes and uncertainty. Never optimise retention at the expense of trust, rest or autonomy. Never invent testimonials, statistics, customer numbers or features.

## Priority order

1. Audit the real app (see `audit-2026-10-24.md`).
2. Perfect the first five minutes: invitation, consent, conversation, first personal insight.
3. Make Today exceptional.
4. Make the constellation emotionally meaningful, every star something actually lived.
5. Build the marketing site around the real product (shipped: site/index.html).
6. Measure activation and retention, in a privacy-respecting way, before scaling acquisition.

The central call: keep the identity deliberately restrained while making the personal insights unusually specific. Being genuinely understood is the memorable thing.
