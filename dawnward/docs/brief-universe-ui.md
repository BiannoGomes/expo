# Brief: the Living Universe as the phone interface (paste into Claude Design)

Paste everything below the line into a Claude Design session on the Dawnward Screens canvas, together with screenshots of the Dawnward Voyage artifact (the quality bar).

---

You are styling the **Dawnward app itself**, phone screens at 390x844, on the Dawnward Screens canvas. Read `canvas.json`, the notes, and the installed `ds/dawnward` system first; build on what exists, never replace other sessions' boards.

**The mission:** make the phone interface look like the attached screenshots of the Living Universe, a real, deep, cinematic cosmos, while staying a perfectly usable app. The attached images are the quality bar and the lighting reference. This is not a wallpaper behind cards; the universe is the environment the interface lives in.

**The world:** ground `#0B0E12` to `#06080D`, ink `#ECE9E2`, one gold `#C9A96A`, cool star `#C7D5EE`, nebula `#4A5A7A` (atmosphere only, never on components or text), dawn `#E8A87C` (sky light only). Cormorant Garamond display, Albert Sans body, IBM Plex Mono small uppercase labels. 8px grid, radius 14/22, borders never shadows, glass panels at `rgba(11,14,18,.58)` with blur for anything that floats over the cosmos.

**The celestial language:** stars are lived moments (real magnitude distribution, most faint, few bright, gold only for moments the person marked significant, diffraction crosses on the brightest). Planets are domains of life with their own character: Mind cool and ringed, Body warm and earth-like, Soul quiet and luminous, Future distant and gaining definition. Planets show gentle terminator light and a fresnel rim glow in their own temperature. Orbits are confirmed connections, one quiet gold arc, never a web. Depth comes from layered star fields, atmospheric perspective and parallax, never from flat star sprinkles.

**Phone-first rules:** every board must work as a real screen someone holds. Text over the cosmos sits on the glass panel or over calm dark sky, never over a busy nebula. Primary actions visible without exploring. Touch targets 44px. A scale label in mono ("Scale 1 · The intimate sky") orients the person. Every screen has a reduced-motion-complete still state.

**Boards to style at this level:** Universe Overview (the "My Universe" home), Planet Detail (one domain with its editorial panel and one next action), Planet Journey, Star Memory, Today, Evening Debrief, Future Universe, Shareable Universe (1080x1920). Elevate the existing Today/Debrief/You boards to match rather than replacing their content.

**Hard laws:** no scores, percentages, streaks, badges, meters, red dots, guilt copy, light theme, or AI-telling language. No em-dashes in copy. A planet never shrinks or darkens for inactivity. Progress is qualitative only: atmosphere, definition, detail. Copy you invent gets a gray invented-fixture note like the existing ones.

**Quality test:** each board should be recognisable as Dawnward with the wordmark covered, beautiful enough to screenshot and share, and still obviously an app someone can use. If a board reads as a space-game HUD or a template, it is wrong.

**Hand back:** the updated canvas plus a short note of boards changed and added, invented copy, and any engineering questions.
