# The Wisdom Library and the first Wisdom Constellation

**Status:** 2026-11-01. Built and tested (`docs/galactic/test/e2e-wisdom.cjs`, 22 checks; server catalog test). Nothing deployed.
**Everything here is a draft.** The sources are well-known published books and papers, written from memory, not re-checked online.
A reviewer must check every source and evidence note before launch, as the brief asks ("verify the primary source before attributing").

## What was built, and what wasn't (the brief's own quality gate)
The brief says: don't add more experts or worlds until **one complete learning journey** has passed testing and user evaluation. So this builds:

1. **The Wisdom Library** (Knowledge galaxy, on the Tree of Life where the planned "Human Mind" was; `#/knowledge/wisdom`, free).
   21 thinkers, each with: discipline, source, the idea in Dawnward's own words, an **evidence class**, its **limits**, a Dawnward world,
   an original question, and one small thing to try. **Reflect on this** opens Reflect in that world on that question, and the question is kept with the answer.
2. **One Wisdom Constellation: Becoming Consistent** (Future world, and the top of the library).
   Question → four perspectives, each labelled by evidence, and where they disagree → choose what usually goes wrong → a seven-day action →
   Home asks how it went → Journey. This is the brief's Discover → Question → Explore → Choose → Act → Review → Integrate.
3. **The brief's questions** in Reflect's question bank (Another question), word for word.

Not built: the other three constellations, the four experiences (Mirror of Identity, Hall of Unlived Lives, Garden of Connection, The Last Sunrise) as guided missions,
the AI, and the new galaxies (below). Those should wait for the gate.

## Evidence classes (never shown as equal)
The page is grouped into three sections, in this order, with a heading for each:

| Class | Label in the app | Who's in it now |

The research notes rely on: Gollwitzer and Sheeran's 2006 meta-analysis of implementation intentions; Lally and colleagues (2010) on habit formation (median 66 days, wide range);
Goyal and colleagues (2014, JAMA Internal Medicine) on meditation programmes; Sisk and colleagues (2018) and Yeager and colleagues (2019) on growth mindset;
Credé, Tynan and Harms (2017) on grit; Kross and colleagues (2014) on distanced self-talk; replication problems in priming research (relevant to parts of Kahneman's book).

## Rules this follows
- **No implied partnership.** The page says "None of these people is connected with Dawnward." No one is shown as an instructor, and no AI speaks as them.
- **Own words only.** Nothing is copied from Mindvalley courses, scripts, recordings or branded sequences. The one quotation is Frankl quoting Nietzsche, attributed as such.
- **Disagreements are shown.** Becoming Consistent says plainly that Clear and Frankl point different ways, and lets you choose by what usually goes wrong for you.
- **Every idea ends in an action.** Each card has a question and something small to try; the constellation ends in a seven-day experiment and a review.
