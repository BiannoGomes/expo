# Dawnward: the chapter art brief (every chapter, every transition, the UI kit)

**Purpose:** art for every chapter written so far and every one planned, plus the transitions between places and a glass-and-gold interface kit. The aim is a surreal, immersive, layered universe.

**Guide images:** the Mindfinity board and the portal image (`design/references/`).

**Spirit:** no crystals. Spirit is now the Observatory of Being: nebula veils, slow halos of light, stillness, starlight on water.

**This replaces** prompts A1 and A2 in `chatgpt-asset-brief.md`. The rest of that brief still stands.

## How to work through it

1. **Use one ChatGPT chat per section** (UI kit, transitions, then one chat per expedition). Long chats drift in style.
2. **Start each chat with Step 0,** and attach two of your earlier images plus the Mindfinity board, so everything matches.
3. **Paste one prompt at a time.** If it's wrong, say "Again, same prompt", and don't describe changes.
4. **Download each image named by its code,** for example `BO1-H.png`. Send them back in batches, and I'll place every one.

You are the art director for Dawnward, an app where a person's inner life is a living universe of five worlds (Mind, Body, Soul, Spirit, Future), other galaxies, a Quantum Gateway to imagine a possible future self, and Earth, where it all comes back into real life. Match the attached images.

Light only where it really falls: warm gold #E6C07B, orbital blue #5B8CFF, cyan #37D5E8, violet #9B5BFF, lilac #C9B8FF, teal #5FE3C0, amber #F0C987. Fine film grain. No plastic CGI, no lens-flare spam, no neon outlines, no cartoons.
Light: one warm golden source from the upper left, a soft rim light on far edges.
People: only seen from behind, or as soft silhouettes of light. Never a detailed face. Never a real or recognisable person. No religious symbols of any specific faith.
Never put any text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, icons or watermarks in an image unless I ask for a blank interface element.

## Formats (the codes say which one to use)

| Code ends in | What it is | Format |
|---|---|---|
| `-H` | Hero: the chapter's opening scene | Portrait 1080 × 1920. Focus in the top 40%; the lower 55% calm and dark, because text sits there. |
| `-B` `-M` `-F` | Optional depth layers for the hero: back, middle, front, so the scene moves in layers as you scroll or tilt | Same size as the hero. `-B` is a full painting; `-M` and `-F` are transparent PNGs holding only their layer. |
| `-X` | The exercise backdrop (sorting, lab, pause, choices) | Portrait 1080 × 1920. Softer and darker than the hero: it sits behind buttons. |
| `-R` | Reflection: the quiet moment of writing | Portrait 1080 × 1920. Very calm and very dark, one small light. |
| `-K` | Reward: the chapter's moon lighting up | Square 1024 × 1024, transparent. |
| `-W` | Worksheet header, on screen only (printing stays plain) | Landscape 1600 × 500. The right 60% dark and empty. |

**To get depth layers, ask like this** after making a hero:

```
Now split this exact scene into three layers, same size and framing: B = the full background painting with the foreground removed; M = only the middle-distance elements on a transparent background; F = only the nearest foreground elements on a transparent background.
```

## What I do with them

- **Heroes and layers** open each chapter. The layers move at different speeds for depth, and stay still when reduced motion is on.
- **Exercise and reflection images** sit behind the interactions.
- **Moons** light up in the solar systems when a chapter is saved.
- **Worksheet headers** go on screen only.
- **Transitions** play between places, always skippable.
- **The UI kit** becomes the panels, buttons, progress and tabs, with live text on top so everything stays readable, translatable and accessible.
