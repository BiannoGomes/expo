# Dawnward: the art brief for ChatGPT (round 2)

> **Update:** Spirit is now made without crystals. Use S1 and S2 in [`chatgpt-chapter-art-brief.md`](chatgpt-chapter-art-brief.md) instead of A1 and A2 below.

Everything the app still needs, in the order it's needed. Each prompt is ready to paste.

## How to do it

1. **Start one new ChatGPT chat** and paste **Step 0** (the style lock) first.
2. **Attach two of your existing images** to the same message, so the new art matches the old. Use the Mind planet and the Mind close-up you made before (or any planet and close-up from round 1).
3. **Paste the prompts one at a time,** in order. If one comes back wrong, say "Again, same prompt" rather than describing changes. Small corrections drift the style.
4. **Bring them back here** as PNGs, a few at a time, and tell me the code for each (for example "A1"). I'll convert them, check the sizes and backgrounds, record their source, and put them in the app.

**What ChatGPT can't make:** sound. Ambient soundscapes are a separate step, and I'll suggest the tool when we get there.

## Step 0: the style lock (paste first, once)

```
You are the art director for Dawnward, an app where a person's inner life is a living universe: five worlds (Mind, Body, Soul, Spirit, Future), other galaxies to explore, a Quantum Gateway to imagine a possible future self, and Earth, where they bring it back into real life. Keep every image in this chat in one consistent style, matching the two images I've attached.

Style: cinematic, painterly sci-fi realism, like a high-end film matte painting. Deep space #05070D to #0A1426. Glowing accents only where light really falls: warm gold #E6C07B, orbital blue #5B8CFF, cyan #37D5E8, violet #9B5BFF, lilac #C9B8FF. Fine film grain. No plastic CGI look, no lens-flare spam, no neon outlines, no cartoon style.
Light: one warm golden light source, from the upper left, with a soft rim light on the far edges.
People: only ever seen from behind or as a soft silhouette of light. Never a detailed face. Never a real or recognisable person.
Never put any text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, buttons, icons, frames or watermarks in any image.
When I ask for "pure black", everything outside the subject must be exactly black, #000000, with no stars, haze or glow reaching the edges.
```

## B. The Quantum Gateway journey (the next thing I build)

The sequence: intention → portal → passage → a possible future self → decision → action → Return to Earth.

It's always skippable. With reduced motion on, these become still images.

**B1. Intention**
```
B1. Portrait, 1080 × 1920. A person seen from behind, small in the frame, standing on a dark stone platform at the edge of space. Ahead, a faint unlit ring hangs in the dark, waiting. Mood: quiet resolve. Focus in the top 45%; the bottom 50% dark so text can sit over it.
```

**B2. Portal forming**
```
B2. Portrait, 1080 × 1920. The same platform and the same ring, now igniting: streams of violet, magenta and gold light spiralling inward to a bright golden point. The person, seen from behind, is lit by it. Focus in the top 50%; the bottom 45% darker.
```

**B3. The passage** (used as a moving tunnel, so the centre matters)
```
B3. Square, 2048 × 2048. Looking straight down a tunnel through spacetime: concentric rings and streaks of violet, magenta, blue and gold light rushing outward from one bright golden point at the exact centre. Radially balanced all the way round, so the image can be slowly rotated and zoomed without a visible seam or a direction. Fill the whole square to the edges. No platform, no people, no horizon.
```

**B4. A possible future self**

B4. Portrait, 1080 × 1920. **B5. The Future Self Observatory**

**B6. Return to Earth**

## Checklist before sending each one back

- **No text** anywhere: no letters, numbers or symbols that look like writing.
- **No faces:** people are seen from behind, or as light.
- **Edges:** "pure black" images are truly black at all four edges.
- **Size and shape** match the prompt: square or portrait.
- **It matches the attached style:** same light from the upper left, same palette, same grain.
