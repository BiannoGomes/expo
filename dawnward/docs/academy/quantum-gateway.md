## A possible you

**Objective:** Imagine one possible future self, then choose one real first step toward it.

**Time:** about 10 minutes

**Safety note:** This is imagination, not a prediction: nobody can see your future, and this doesn't try. If thinking about the future feels heavy right now, stop at any step. findahelpline.com lists free, confidential support lines for your country.

### The Quantum Gateway (intro, optional)

Here you'll imagine one possible version of yourself, a year from now, who moved toward something you care about. It's imagination, not a prediction. Then you'll look honestly at where you are, compare two paths, and choose one small, real step. Nothing here is scored.


### Your intention (rehearse)

**Prompt:** What would you like to move toward? One sentence is enough.

**Example offered (never prefilled):** Feeling at ease speaking up in meetings. / Starting the course I keep putting off.


### The gateway (portal, optional)

When you're ready, open it. The passage takes a few seconds, and you can skip it.


### A possible you (reflect)

**Prompt:** Imagine a version of you, a year from now, who moved toward this. They're not a prediction, just one possibility. What might they say to you today?

**Example offered (never prefilled):** Start smaller than you think. The first step was easier than the worrying.


### The Mirror (reflect)

**Prompt:** Back to now. What's true about where you are today? Only what you know, not what you fear.

**Example offered (never prefilled):** I speak up in small groups, but not when my manager is there.


### The Crossroads (lab)

Two or three paths from where you are now, weighed against what matters to you.

**Prompt:** Write two or three paths, including a smaller version. Then name what matters most to you here.

**Afterwards:** There's no total here, on purpose. Look for a path that helps with what matters, at a cost you could live with. A smaller version is often the way through.

**Example offered (never prefilled):** Ask to present at the next team meeting / Share one idea in the weekly call / Not yet: after the project ends / Confidence / Keeping my workload calm


### The First Step (plan, optional)

**Prompt:** If you want, choose one small, real step toward that path, and when.

**Example offered (never prefilled):** Write down one idea before Thursday's call and say it.


### Return to Earth (close, optional)

You imagined a possible you, looked honestly at now, and chose a path. The future isn't fixed; this was one possibility, and your first step is real. Your letter, A Letter From a Possible You, stays here whenever you want it.
