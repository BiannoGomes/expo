# Dawnward audit, 2026-10-24

Phase 1 of the growth directive: what is actually true in the repository today, ranked by severity. Everything marked **verified** was checked against the code this day; items marked **verify** need a hands-on pass before being treated as fact.

## What is verifiably strong

- **Tokens are law.** `apps/mobile/lib/theme.ts` carries the exact canonical values; screens consume tokens, not improvised values. (verified)
- **Reduced motion is respected** across all four motion modules (sky, constellation, motion, debrief listening). (verified)
- **The safety gate is green** on real models: crisis recall 7 of 7, structural checks pass, hardening shipped as `8a283128`. (verified, remote)
- **Voice is never stored**: `/transcribe` passes audio through transcription and discards it. (verified earlier this cycle)
- **Erase everything** exists in settings, and consent-with-name lives at the onboarding door. (verified)
- **The forbidden list holds**: no streaks, badges, scores, guilt copy, red dots or light theme anywhere in the app. (verified by review across this cycle)
- **The marketing site now matches the product** (rebuilt this day from real 3x captures). (verified)

## Critical path (not code: owner actions)

1. **Hosting is not deployed.** The server runs only in development containers. Railway is chosen and prepared; waiting on the Hobby plan unlock, project creation and `RAILWAY_TOKEN`. Everything after (store build, TestFlight) queues behind this.
2. **Store accounts.** Apple Developer enrollment is the longest external clock and has not started.

## High

3. **The weekly reveal exists in design only.** The canvas board `Reveal` (day 14, the revision, gold commitment stars) is canon, but the app has no reveal route; `app/` holds tabs (Today, Debrief, You) plus campaign, settings and onboarding. The fortnight payoff, the product's most distinctive moment, is unbuilt. (verified)
4. **The star-birth moment is weaker in app than in design.** The canvas `Witnessed` board defines the sequence: a beat of dark, stars born gold and cooling to ink, lines drawing, the day's closing line rising. The app debrief answers with text and the goodnight, and the constellation lives on You; the witnessed sky moment itself is not implemented. (verified at route level; animation-level detail to verify)
5. **Offline and rest states are designed but not built.** Canvas boards `TodayOffline`, `TodayRest`, `DebriefOffline`, `DebriefVoiceDeclined`, `DebriefRest` define honest copy for every degraded state; the app handles fetch failures with generic catches and has no rest-day presentation. (verified: catches only in index/debrief)

## Medium

6. **Accessibility labels are thin outside the debrief.** debrief.tsx carries 5 label/role usages and the Press primitive provides role and state everywhere, but index.tsx has 2 and you.tsx none; the constellation and belief cards need a labels pass. (verified by count; full screen-reader run not yet done)
7. **No measurement exists at all.** The directive's Phase 5 needs activation and retention signals; the brand forbids surveillance-feeling analytics. A decision is needed: consent-gated, aggregate-only counts (onboarding completion, D7/D30, debrief completion), no behavioral SDKs, documented in the privacy page. Until then, every growth experiment lacks a readout.
8. **Notification parity with the lock-screen designs.** `lib/presence.ts` schedules named notifications with deep links; the canvas Lock boards fix the two sacred times and copy. Confirm schedule times and wording match. (verify)

## Low

9. **Campaign "let it rest" sheet**: designed on canvas (`CampaignRest`), presence in app to confirm. (verify)
10. **Site hosting**: the rebuilt landing page ships with the repo but has no public URL until the Railway deploy (it can ride the same service as a static mount, or any static host).

## Recommended build order (after hosting unblocks)

1. The weekly reveal screen (finding 3), reusing `lib/constellation.tsx`, fixtures from the canvas board.
2. The witnessed star-birth sequence in the debrief (finding 4), the canvas board is the spec, within the three sanctioned motion moments (it is one of them).
3. Offline and rest states (finding 5), copy already written on the canvas boards.
4. Accessibility labels pass (finding 6).
5. The measurement decision (finding 7), then the growth experiments in the directive's order.
