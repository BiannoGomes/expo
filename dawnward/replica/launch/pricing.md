# Pricing (replica-launch): for the owner to decide

Nothing here is live, and no Stripe or App Store products were created.

## What the five charge

All prices were read 2026-10-31 in US dollars. Sources are in `../recon/<app>.md`.

| App | Free | Monthly | Yearly | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rosebud | Free plan retired 2026-09-30 (help centre), though the home page still says free | $12.99 | $107.99 | Higher tiers $24.99 and $59.99; weekly usage caps |
| Mindsera | Yes, limited, with one-time tries of AI features | $14.99 | $129 | Trial length not stated |
| Reflection | Yes, generous, with a daily AI allowance | $8.00 | $69.00 | Lifetime $249.99; 7-day trial |
| Stoic | Journaling free | Premium about $6.99; Premium + AI about $12.99 | about $99.99 for Premium + AI | Lifetime $299; 3-day AI trial complaints |
| Exist | 30-day trial, card required | $6.99 on the web, $8.49 as an iOS in-app purchase | $62.90 | One plan |

## What reviewers said

- 57 reviews mention price and 12 are about billing (`../feedback.md`).
- The angriest complaints aren't about the price itself. They're about:
  - caps on something already paid for (Rosebud);
  - a short trial that turns into a yearly charge, and refused refunds (Stoic);
  - repeated upsell pop-ups (Reflection, Mindsera);
  - privacy features locked behind Premium (Reflection).

## Recommendation (needs the owner's yes)

1. **One plan, no usage caps.** A debrief is never cut off partway.
2. **Free always includes:**
   - the evening debrief;
   - the morning steps;
   - Your evenings (read, search, leave out);
   - export, deletion, and app lock once it exists.
   Privacy is never a paid feature.
3. **Paid adds the deeper layers.** For example: the weekly review, Ask your evenings, and the worlds beyond Mind.
4. **Price in the $6.99 to $8.99 a month range,** below Rosebud and Mindsera. Yearly about 2 months free.
5. **Trial: 14 days, no surprise charge.**
   - A reminder 3 days before it ends.
   - Cancel in one tap.
   - Refunds handled kindly.
6. **One calm moment to ask for payment,** never pop-ups or banners, and never during a reflection.

This is a draft for discussion. The figures are not tested with anyone.
