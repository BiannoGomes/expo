# Landing page outline (replica-launch): draft, not built, not published

Built on angle A from `../fixes.md`: trust, in a calm tone. Every claim on the page must already be true in the shipped app.

## Sections

1. **Hero.**
   - Headline: "Talk about your day. Wake up to three steps from your own words."
   - One line: "Every step shows the evening it came from."
   - One button.
   - A real screenshot of Today with a quote, taken from a real build with labelled sample content.
2. **The problem**, in plain words with no reviewer quotes:
   - Journaling apps that forget you, or remember you wrong.
   - Apps that turn reflection into a streak to protect.
3. **How it works**, in three steps:
   - Talk or type tonight.
   - Three steps in the morning, each with its sentence.
   - Correct anything that's wrong.
4. **What's different.** Each point is tied to a built fix:
   - Steps quote your evenings, checked word for word (F1).
   - Read and search every evening (F2).
   - Leave any evening out and bring it back (F3).
   - See and correct everything it believes about you.
5. **The four worlds.** Mind, Body, Soul, Future: one visual, no numbers.
6. **Privacy, stated once, in plain words:**
   - which AI provider is used;
   - what leaves the phone;
   - whether anything trains a model;
   - how to export and delete.
   - Gaps to close first: three competitors had these facts scattered across pages or inconsistent. Write this paragraph only after the existing `site/privacy.html` has been checked against the processors actually used.
7. **Pricing.** Only after the owner decides (`pricing.md`).
8. **FAQ:**
   - Is it therapy? No.
   - What happens if I miss a week? The next morning is lighter.
   - Can I take my words with me? Yes, all of them.

## Rules carried from research

- No ratings, user counts, "trusted by" rows or percentages unless they're real and linked.
- No competitor's name anywhere.
- Every claim is checked by the dawnward-product-fidelity skill before anything is shown publicly.
