Dawnward

What you share stays yours

Last updated 28 September 2026

Dawnward exists to help you become who you're becoming. To do that well, it holds real things about your life: your reflections, your plans, your fears, sometimes your health and the people you love. This page says plainly what happens with all of it.

What Dawnward keeps

  • Your words. Your onboarding conversation, your evening debriefs, and your corrections. These build your Personal Model, and nothing else.
  • What Dawnward comes to believe about you, always with the evidence it came from, always correctable by you.
  • Your settings. Your name if you choose to give one, your daily times, your preferences.

What Dawnward never does

  • Your data is never sold and never used for advertising.
  • Your reflections never train anyone else's AI models. They are used for one purpose: understanding you, for you.
  • Voice recordings are not kept. When you speak a debrief, the audio is turned into words and discarded. Only the words remain, and you see them before anything is saved.
  • If you ever write something that suggests you are in crisis, Dawnward responds with care and support resources, and stores nothing from that moment.

Your rights, built in

These are not a request form. They are buttons in the app, in Settings, working today:

  • Correct. Dispute any belief the app holds about you. Your word wins, every time.
  • Export. Download everything Dawnward knows, with its evidence, in one file.
  • Erase. Delete your account and every trace of it, permanently, in one action.

Consent, properly

Because your reflections can include sensitive things (health, emotions, beliefs), Dawnward asks for your explicit consent before your story begins, explains exactly what it covers, and lets you withdraw it at any time in Settings. Data is processed under the GDPR; sensitive data is handled under Article 9 with your explicit consent as the basis, and stored in the European Union.

Who else touches the data

Two processors, doing one job each: an AI provider (Anthropic) that reads your words to write your plans and reflections back, and a transcription provider (ElevenLabs) that turns your voice into words and keeps neither. Both are bound by agreements that forbid using your data for anything else, including training.

Questions

Write to privacy@dawnward.app and a person will answer.

This policy is written to be read. A formal notice with the full legal detail (controller identity, retention periods, supervisory authority) accompanies it and is reviewed by counsel before public launch.
