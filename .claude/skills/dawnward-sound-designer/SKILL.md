# Dawnward sound designer

- Interactive (`src/sound.js`): muted by default; the first tap resumes the AudioContext inside the gesture (iOS). The owner's score is the bed; a low-pass filter opens with story scale (380 Hz to 16 kHz), closes slightly on world focus; each world answers with a soft sine bell. Pauses when the tab is hidden.
- Film (`film/make-audio.sh`): score + narrator Naomi (Higgsfield preset, lines in `marketing/fx/vo5`), music ducked by sidechain, the bed lifted 4 dB under the quiet opening so the first line never lands in silence, dialogue limited to -1.5 dBFS, a quiet effects stem from `film/make-sfx.py` (typing ticks, the lift, the crossing, a chime as each world is named), two-pass loudnorm to -14 LUFS, true peak about -1.2 dB. Writes dialogue, music and effects stems.
- Narration only in quiet sections; start lines just after a beat so hits do not mask first syllables.
- Silence is a design element. No whooshes. Verify with ebur128 and per-line voice-to-music ratio.
- Rights: score generated by the owner on Suno; narration generated on the owner's Higgsfield account. Check plan terms before paid media.
